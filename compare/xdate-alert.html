Where FreshAuthority wins

All 50 states, the earliest file, and a guarantee with a number on it

XDate stops at 47 states and ranks signals in a dashboard. FreshAuthority covers every state, commits to 6:00 AM ET in your inbox, hands you the actual grant rows with full contacts (usable by factoring and dispatch teams too), includes renewals and lapses in every tier rather than treating timing as the whole product, and guarantees ≥95% contactability — or the month is free. During the trial you get the real files, CSV included.

Honest case for XDate Alert

You want ranked signals across their states, with zero card risk

If you write in XDate's 47 covered states, $39 for all of them is a strong price, the no-card trial plus the forgot-to-cancel refund makes starting painless, and their switch-likelihood ranking is backed by published measurements (they report about 35% of carriers move insurer at their first renewal). If ranked signals beat raw files for your workflow, XDate is a fair pick — it just isn't the daily grants file.
