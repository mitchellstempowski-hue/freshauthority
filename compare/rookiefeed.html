Where FreshAuthority wins

Same morning list, a fraction of the price, with the trigger files

Both feeds are rebuilt each morning from the same FMCSA grants — RookieFeed counted 6,271 grants in the 30 days to October 29; our pipeline counted 6,193 in the same window (small scope differences). FreshAuthority adds the renewals and lapses files, delivers an hour earlier in Eastern terms, guarantees ≥95% contactability, and charges $49 for every state instead of $149/$599. The 7-day trial is free instead of $19.

Honest case for RookieFeed

You want weekly-only, or their extras

RookieFeed's $59 Weekly plan suits agents who only want a Monday digest of one state, and their dashboard keeps 31 days of downloads with first-time-authority and application filters. If you prefer a lighter cadence and their tooling, the Weekly plan is a fair product — it just isn't the daily file.
