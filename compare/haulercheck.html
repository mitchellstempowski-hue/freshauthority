Where FreshAuthority wins

Earlier delivery, lower price, guaranteed accuracy

If you work the list in the morning, FreshAuthority is in your inbox up to three hours earlier (6 AM ET vs ~8 AM CT), costs $10–$70/mo less per tier, and is the only one of the two that guarantees the data: if fewer than 95% of rows have a working phone or email, the month is free. In our October 29 build run, 99.97% of grant rows carried a phone or an email.

Honest case for HaulerCheck

You want registrations too, or a research tool

HaulerCheck folds new USDOT registrations into the same file and links every row to a full carrier report with risk level and change history — closer to a research platform than a pure lead file. If you vet carriers (brokers, factoring risk teams) as much as you prospect them, that breadth is genuinely useful, and their free carrier check covers 750,000+ companies.
