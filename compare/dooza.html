Where FreshAuthority wins

You want the data daily, without a sales call

New authorities are a same-week opportunity. A daily file by 6 AM ET reaches carriers while they are still shopping; a Monday list reaches some of them five days later. FreshAuthority also starts free (sample or 7-day trial) instead of paid-first, needs no call to buy, and guarantees the contacts at ≥95% or the month is free — for $39/mo instead of $129 every 4 weeks.

Honest case for Dooza

You want pre-filing applicants, or calls done for you

Dooza's angle is genuinely different: they target carriers who applied and have no liability filing yet — earlier in the buying window than a granted-authority list reaches. And their booked-calls add-on suits an agency that would rather pay for appointments than dial. If either of those is the product you actually want, Dooza is built for it.
