How to choose

You sell trucking insurance

The renewal file is where your second sale lives, so insist on insurer + policy date on every row and a renewals list you can work as its own campaign. FreshAuthority and HaulerCheck both bundle renewals and lapses at every tier; Dooza leads with renewals but delivers weekly; AuthorityAlerts folds renewal windows into a digest. If morning speed matters — it should, everyone works the same carriers — compare delivery times in your timezone, not theirs.

You factor freight or dispatch

You need grants fast, with fleet size and cargo to filter. FreshAuthority ($79 every state, ~1.3¢ per carrier) and HaulerCheck ($99, adds registration data and carrier reports) are the two daily nationwide files. RookieFeed covers the same grants at $599/mo nationwide.

Budget is the whole decision

AuthorityAlerts at $20/mo on their own site is the cheapest daily look at this data, with a no-card trial. You give up the 6 AM delivery, the separate renewal file, and any accuracy guarantee — a fair trade for some buyers, stated plainly.

You want it done for you

Dooza will call your list and book quote calls ($499 per 4 weeks for up to 4 calls), and AuthorityAlerts' Outreach tier emails carriers in your name (from $499/mo). FreshAuthority doesn't do outreach for you — we sell the file, you work it. If you'd rather buy appointments than leads, those two are the honest picks.

What to verify before you pay anyone (including us)

Ask for a sample from your state and check ten rows by hand against FMCSA's public lookup: is the insurer right, does the phone ring the carrier, is the grant date real? Check whether renewals and lapses cost extra. Check the delivery time in Eastern — a file that lands at 10:30 AM reaches you after the 6 AM subscribers have already dialed. And ask what happens when the data is wrong: FreshAuthority's answer is written down — below 95% contactable, the month is free.
