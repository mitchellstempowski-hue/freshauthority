Questions, answered straight

What are new trucking authority leads?

New trucking authority leads are motor carriers that FMCSA has just granted operating authority — new MC numbers that must buy commercial trucking insurance before they can legally haul freight. FreshAuthority compiles the FMCSA new authority list every day and delivers it as a clean CSV by 6 AM ET, with the carrier's current insurer, phone, and email on every row.

Where does the FMCSA new authority list come from?

From FMCSA's own public records — the Licensing & Insurance files and the motor carrier census published by the U.S. Department of Transportation. FreshAuthority compiles, cleans, and deduplicates those records daily; you are paying for the daily compilation, the trigger files, and the 6 AM ET delivery, not for the raw public data.
