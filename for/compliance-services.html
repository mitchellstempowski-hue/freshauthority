Why the timing works

The setup window

Everything is un-bought

In their first weeks, new carriers have chosen almost none of their compliance vendors. Consortium, ELD, filing help — the whole stack is an open decision, once.

The audit clock

New entrants are monitored from day one

New carriers operate under new-entrant monitoring and face a safety audit early on. "Be ready before they come" is a concrete, deadline-backed pitch — not a scare tactic.

Owner-direct

Small fleets, direct contact

Most new authorities are one-to-five-truck operations. The filed phone — cell-flagged in the file — reaches the person who will actually buy the compliance package.

How compliance services use FreshAuthority

Daily prospecting

Each morning's grants are first-time businesses in setup mode — the exact audience for consortium enrollment, authority-service packages and audit prep.

Trigger follow-ups

The lapse file lists carriers whose liability filing was cancelled — carriers at risk of revocation who urgently need filing help to stay legal.

Renewal timing

The renewals file shows when a carrier's policy year turns — a natural moment for annual compliance reviews and service renewals of your own.
