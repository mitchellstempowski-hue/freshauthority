Every row carries the fields a factor actually filters on — no enrichment step, no second vendor. The reps who win new authorities don't call 6,193 carriers; they call the 300 that look like their book:

Fleet size: power_units

Separate the first-truck startups (fastest decisions, no incumbent factor, highest lifetime value per dollar of effort) from the 5–50 truck fleets your underwriters prefer. Sort descending when you want funded volume this quarter; ascending when you want day-one relationships.

Cargo: cargo_or_equipment

General freight, autos, produce, household goods, building materials — cut to the freight types your risk team already understands, and skip the rest without opening a single row by hand.

Geography: state files or the national cut

Run Every State and filter to your lanes, or take One State files for the regions your reps actually work. New grants are sorted newest first, so the morning's call list starts with carriers whose authority posted yesterday.

Contacts that reach the owner

Phone is flagged cell when it matches the carrier's census cell-phone field — the number that reaches the owner-operator, not a switchboard. Email is the address the carrier filed with FMCSA. And the contact data is guaranteed: below 95% contactable in a file, that month is free.

What's in every row

Sixteen fields, insurer and BIPD effective date first: insurer name, policy effective date, legal name and DBA, USDOT and MC numbers, street/city/state/ZIP, phone (cell-flagged), email, power units, cargo/equipment, and the authority grant date.
