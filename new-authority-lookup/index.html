Questions, answered plainly

Is the New Trucking Authority Lookup free?

Yes. State counts, top cities and masked sample rows are free with no form and no account, and they stay free. The paid files and the free 25-lead sample carry full phone and email fields.

Where does the lookup data come from?

Public records published by the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA). Counts are computed from the FMCSA file dated November 13, 2026 and refreshed as new files publish.

What does 'granted yesterday' mean?

Carriers whose operating authority grant date is the newest date in the current FMCSA file (November 13, 2026). 'This week' is the 7 calendar days ending on that date, and the 30-day count is the full trailing-30-day file.
