Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026. FreshAuthority is operated by MS ECOM LLC. This page says plainly what we collect, why, and who processes it. We do not sell your personal information.

What we collect

Information you give us. When you request a free sample, sign up for state alerts, start a trial, subscribe, or email us, we collect the details you submit — typically your name, work email, company, and the state(s) you work. Form submissions on this site are delivered to us through Web3Forms.

Payment information. Subscriptions are billed by Stripe. Your card details go directly to Stripe; we never see or store full card numbers. We keep the billing records Stripe gives us (plan, status, last four digits) to run your subscription.

Basic technical logs. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and operation. This site itself sets no tracking or advertising cookies.

About the lead data itself

The product we sell is compiled from public records published by the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA) and the U.S. Department of Transportation — the business contact details carriers and their insurers file with the federal government. Our methodology page documents the sources and the build. If you are a carrier and want to correct your federal filing, that record is maintained by FMCSA; we rebuild from the newest federal release every delivery morning, so corrections propagate on the next run.

How we use what we collect

To deliver your sample, alerts, trial, and subscription files; to bill and manage your subscription; to reply to your messages; and to operate and improve the service. We do not use your information for third-party advertising, and we do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone.

Who processes it

A short list of service providers, each handling data only to provide its function: Stripe (payments and subscription billing), Zoho (our email and file delivery), Web3Forms (website form intake), and GitHub Pages (website hosting). We may also disclose information if required by law.

Email

Every email we send carries our postal address and a way to opt out, and opt-outs are honored. Transactional email about your own sample, trial, or subscription (delivery notices, billing receipts) is part of the service itself.

Retention and your choices

We keep sample, alert, and subscription records for as long as needed to run the service and keep ordinary business records. You can ask us at any time what we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing mitchell@msecomllc.com. We reply within one business day.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
