Methodology & source

Source: public filings published by the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA) and the U.S. Department of Transportation. FreshAuthority's pipeline reads the FMCSA files daily (files out by 6 AM ET) and records each for-hire motor carrier authority grant with its grant date and the carrier's state.

How this index is built: daily grant files are trailing-30-day windows, so monthly counts are computed from the deduplicated union of every daily file on record (deduplicated by USDOT number and grant date), grouped by calendar month of the grant date and state. A month is published as complete only when the record covers every day of it. Coverage on record begins September 30, 2026, so September 2026 — of which a single day is covered — is deliberately not published as a month. October 2026 is the first complete month. FreshAuthority is not affiliated with FMCSA or USDOT, and no government endorsement is implied.

What is not here: no carrier names, phone numbers or emails are published in this index — only aggregate counts. Figures are counts of authority grants as filed, not a count of trucks, drivers or active businesses.

Questions, answered plainly

What is the New Authority Formation Index?

A free monthly count of new FMCSA for-hire trucking operating authorities, by state, published by FreshAuthority on this permanent page. The latest complete month is October 2026: 6,375 grants in the 50 states.

Where does the data come from?

Public filings published by the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA). FreshAuthority's pipeline reads the FMCSA files daily; files are out by 6 AM ET. Counts here are monthly aggregates of authority grant dates in those filings.

Why is there no growth ranking in the first edition?

Honest history: our records support one complete month so far. Month-over-month rankings begin with the next edition rather than being estimated from partial data.

Can I cite or reuse the Index?

Yes — the aggregate state counts are a free asset. Please cite FreshAuthority's New Authority Formation Index with a link to this page. The downloadable CSV contains aggregate counts only; carrier contact files are a separate paid product.

What history exists so far?

One complete month (October 2026), plus November 2026 month-to-date through November 8, 2026.
