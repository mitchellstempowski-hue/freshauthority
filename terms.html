The service

FreshAuthority compiles daily lead files — new operating-authority grants, insurance renewals, and lapsed-liability carriers — from public records published by the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA) and the U.S. Department of Transportation, and delivers them by email by 6:00 AM ET, Monday through Saturday. The build method and its limits are documented on our methodology page.

Free trial

New subscribers get a 7-day free trial. No card is required to start the trial. If you continue into a paid plan, billing begins on day 8 at the plan price you selected.

Plans, billing, and renewal

All States is $49/month or $490/year. Plus Feeds is $99/month or $990/year. Subscriptions are billed through Stripe and renew automatically at the end of each billing period until cancelled. Prices may change with notice before your next renewal; a change never applies mid-period.

Cancellation and refunds

You may cancel at any time — by email to mitchell@msecomllc.com or through the billing link in your Stripe receipts. Cancellation stops future charges; your access runs to the end of the period you have paid for. If you forgot to cancel, we refund the first paid charge in full when you ask within 7 days of it. Beyond that window, charges for periods already delivered are not refundable, except where the guarantee below applies.

The accuracy guarantee

If a delivered month's file falls below 95% of rows carrying a working phone number or email address, that month is free. Coverage is measured on every run and published on our methodology page. To claim the guarantee for a month, email us within 30 days of that month's delivery.
